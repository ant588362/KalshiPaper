   Cause: `gh issue create` made #1, then the very next `gh issue list --search` came back empty (GitHub's

   18:48 decisions never happened.
2. **The trigger is far worse than "a few gaps".** Only **9 runs in 46 hours** (10-27 01:19 → 10-28 23:09).
   That's 8 scheduled runs plus 1 manual, and 1 of the 9 failed. Gaps: 6.3, 6.6, 5.6, 3.7, 5.8, 7.2, 10.4 h.
3. **Momentum/reversion eligibility depends on gap luck.** Markets that had a 5–8 h-old quote, per run:
   `0, 0, 338, 280, 224, 0, 283, 318, 0`. In 4 of 9 runs, **no market could trigger** these bots because
   no previous run fell 5–8 h earlier. In the other 5 runs, the "6-hour move" was really whatever single gap
   happened to land in the window. I am **not** saying this caused the losses. The data can't show that.
4. **Random is out of cash, not capped.** Cash $0.05 with $39.71 open, so it can't trade again until
   something settles. That's not a bug, but its future trade count depends on settlement timing.
5. **Open marks: "no bid" and "missing" both count as $0.** `mark()` values an open position at 0 when its
   quote is missing (API returned nothing), and also when the bid is 0. Reversion's 6 open positions ($16.60
   cost) are marked at $0.42. I checked them live, and all 6 are weather/rain markets still `active` with a bid of
   0–2c, so that's a real near-zero mark, not missing data. The code can't tell the two cases apart, and a
   top-of-book bid isn't what you'd actually get after depth and an exit fee.
6. **Correlated exposure gets past the $6/event cap.** Examples of open cost: favorite **$11.29 on BTC**
   (`KXBTCD` + `KXBTC` are different events, same coin) and **$10.59 on oil** (`KXWTI` + `KXBRENTD`). random has
   about $12.6 on **one college football game** (KENN–MTU spread + total + 1st-half spread are 3 series). The
   $50 cap limits open cost, not lifetime loss. random has already lost $60.24 settled.
7. **Fee gaps:** (a) the fee cache is per event and **never refreshed**; (b) markets carry a
   `fee_waiver_expiration_time` field that's ignored. If a waiver is on, we over-charge, which is the
   pessimistic direction but still wrong; (c) fills aren't stored per price level, so a fee can only be
   re-derived approximately afterwards.
8. **The fingerprint misses most of the engine.** It covers `decide()` + `mid()` + `move()` + params. It does
   **not** cover the shared filter (`tradable`, MIN/MAX_HOURS, MAX_SPREAD, MIN_VOLUME), limits, `fees.py`,
   `plan_buy`, `mark`, or settlement. Editing any of those would quietly mix two experiments in one record.

## 3. NBA pregame consensus-discount test: assessment

**Verdict: the idea is sound to paper-test, but it's blocked on the trigger and on an API key. Not ready to start.**

What checks out (verified 2026-10-28):
- Kalshi has `KXNBAGAME` (fee_type `quadratic_with_maker_fees`, multiplier 1; taker = same 0.07 formula).
  One market per team, `mutually_exclusive: true`. Volume is huge (e.g. 3.2M contracts/24h on MIA–ATL) and spreads are 1c.
- **Your close_time warning is correct:** e.g. KXNBAGAME-26OCT28CHADET closes 2026-10-30 23:00Z but
  `occurrence_datetime`/`expected_expiration_time` = 2026-10-29 02:00Z. Tipoff should come from the odds feed's
  `commence_time`, cross-checked against Kalshi's `occurrence_datetime`. If they differ by more than 15 min, skip as an uncertain match.
- The Odds API (docs + pricing page read 10-28): Starter = **free, 500 credits/month, "most bookmakers",
  no historical**. `/v4/sports/basketball_nba/odds?markets=h2h&regions=us` costs **1 credit**. Empty
  responses cost 0. The US region lists DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Bovada and others. Each market has its own
  `last_update` (the bookmaker-level one is deprecated), so the 5-minute freshness rule can be checked.
- Terms (updated 31 Aug 2026): storing data indefinitely and deriving values is **permitted**. Only reselling
  it as a raw data feed is banned. A private paper test is fine.

Blockers:
1. **API key = account signup.** Your rules say no new accounts. It's a free email signup, but it's an account,
   so it needs his decision (and the adult rule applies: see note below).
2. **Timing can't work on the current trigger.** One check 60–120 min before tipoff, with odds under 5 min old
   and Kalshi depth within 60 s, needs a run *inside* a 60-minute window. With today's 4–10 h gaps most games would be
   missed. Adding cron lines (F8) improves the odds but doesn't guarantee anything. Missed games must be logged as data failures,
   never back-filled.
3. **Coverage untested.** Whether 3+ US books show paired moneylines 60–120 min out on the free plan is
   unknown until the first real call.

Rules as drafted look right. Two additions:
- Use the **same** book's two sides for its no-vig probability (your formula). Drop a book if either side's
  `last_update` is more than 5 min old, then count the books left (need 3 or more).
- Skip when Kalshi's YES ask for team A plus YES ask for team B is under $1.00 or over $1.03. Either means the two
  markets disagree with each other, so something's off.

Credit budget: 12 calls/day × 30 = 360 of 500. Calls only fire when an NBA game tips 60–120 min from now,
so off-days cost 0. Every raw response is saved (gzip JSON) next to the decision, so entries can only use odds
that existed at the time.

Honest expectation: liquid NBA lines on Kalshi track sportsbooks closely. A 5-point cushion over the
**lowest** book estimate after fees will probably trigger rarely or never. "0 qualifying trades" is a valid
result, and it would be the data saying no edge at these settings.
